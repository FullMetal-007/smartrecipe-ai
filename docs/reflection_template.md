# Critical reflection template

Complete this document in your own words after running the system. Remove the prompts and replace them with truthful observations. Do not claim a result that is not supported by your experiment files.

## Technical reflection

- What worked as intended? Refer to specific test IDs and trace stages.
- Which bugs or integration issues occurred, and how did you diagnose them?
- Did the baseline retrieval find a joint ingredient match for T01? What evidence supports your answer?
- Which constraints or ingredient phrases did the parser fail to recognise?
- What did the A/B/C/D comparison show? Include measured values and explain cases where the enhanced approach did not improve results.

## Social reflection

- Who benefits from ingredient-aware recommendations?
- Who could be disadvantaged by incomplete recipe metadata, cultural ingredient vocabulary, cost of ingredients, or limited dietary coverage?
- What feedback from a real human evaluator changed your view of the system?

## Ethical reflection

- What information leaves the local machine when Gemini is enabled?
- How could an incorrect recipe recommendation cause harm or reduce trust?
- Which controls are effective, and which risks remain unresolved?

## Limitations and future improvements

- Identify at least two limitations supported by observed evidence.
- Propose future improvements and state how you would test them.
- Distinguish implemented features from planned work.
